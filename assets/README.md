# Assets (`assets`)

## Purpose

Assets hold durable images, PDFs, diagrams, datasets, and attachments needed
by the archive.

## What belongs here

Store a local asset only when a lawful durable copy adds value. Record its
creator, source, license, and consuming documents when known.
