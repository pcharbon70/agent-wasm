# Archive (`90-archive`)

## Purpose

The archive retains inactive or superseded material whose context remains
useful.

## What belongs here

Move dormant inquiries, obsolete proposals, and replaced documents here when
deletion would erase provenance. State why material was archived and link its
replacement when one exists.
